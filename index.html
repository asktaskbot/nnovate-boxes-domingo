Unboxing is the first impression

NNOVATE builds the moment a brand gets opened. From structure to print, from tissue to the courier at the door, we design, produce, kit and ship custom packaging that feels considered, tactile and unmistakably yours.

And we do not stop at the box: we pack your products inside it, tissue, inserts and cards included. When a launch needs to land, we extend it into PR drops, influencer gifting and custom merch.

Our box index

A working index of the formats we design and produce: mailers, rigid boxes, PR drops and kits. Every format can arrive empty or filled: we pack your products, inserts and cards inside before it ships. Packaging for brands that need more than a container; they need an opening worth filming.

NNOVATE is a box well opened: tissue that folds back right, an insert that says the thing, and a reason to keep the box long after the product is out.

We sketch, sample and fix it in the studio, so the first unboxing you see is never on someone else's camera.

Then we kit it by hand, pack it, and send it out from Los Angeles, one door at a time.

What goes inside

Your products, packed inside.

We do not only make the box. You send us your company’s own products and we pack them in: each item placed, wrapped in tissue, with the insert and card on top, then sealed and shipped. Ready to open.

  1. (01)

    You send your products

    Your own products, in any quantity, arrive at our Los Angeles studio. We log and check every one.

  2. (02)

    We design the box & inserts

    Structure, trays, inserts and cards sized to fit your products exactly.

  3. (03)

    We pack every kit by hand

    Products, tissue, inserts and cards placed the same way in every box, with a quality check.

  4. (04)

    Sealed & shipped

    Sealed, labeled and sent from Los Angeles to your warehouse or straight to each door.

How a box gets made

Four steps, one team, no hand-offs between the idea and the doorstep.

  1. (01) Design

    Structure, dielines, artwork and finish, built around how the box will be opened.

  2. (02) Sample

    Physical prototypes and print proofs before anything goes to volume.

  3. (03) Produce

    Print management, inserts, tissue, ribbon and merch sourced to the same spec.

  4. (04) Kit & ship

    We pack your products into every box by hand, check each kit and ship straight from Los Angeles.
